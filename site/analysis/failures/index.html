Why models fail on cytokine responses

Most hard items are hard because the response was barely measured, not because the models got the biology wrong.

1 · How a model is scored

Each item is one cytokine in one donor's cell type. A model passes if it beats the context's average response.

Correlation is taken over the 2,000 most variable genes.

How each item quantity is computed

Response, ΔMean expression of treated cells minus mean of untreated (PBS) cells, same donor and cell type, per gene (log of counts per 10,000, 2,000 most variable genes).Δ = mean(treated) − mean(control)
Signal-to-noiseSize of the response divided by the size expected if the cytokine did nothing. Near 1: indistinguishable from no effect.‖Δ‖ ÷ √( Σgenes σ²control · (1/ntreated + 1/ncontrol) )
Within-item reliabilitySplit the item's treated and control cells at random into two halves, compute Δ in each, correlate the halves, scale up to full size. Low: the measurement is noisy, even within one donor.2r ÷ (1 + r), r = corr(Δhalf 1, Δhalf 2)
Donor agreementCorrelation of this donor's Δ with the average Δ of the same cytokine and cell type in the other donors. Low: this donor's cells responded differently, or the measurement is noisy.corr(Δdonor, mean Δother donors)
Average-response fitHow well the bar to beat already matches the truth.corr(Δ, Δaverage response)
Receptor expressionMean expression in untreated cells of the least-expressed subunit of the best receptor complex (a cell needs only one working receptor).maxcomplexes minsubunits mean(control)

5 · Noisy items still rank models

Even on unreliable items, stronger models pass more. Noise lowers precision; it does not scramble the order.

6 · Four numbers per model

Pass rate and ability come from the pass rule; the two correlations from the raw predictions.

What this means

For model builders

Read the pass rate on clear items first. Elsewhere, a model can only gain by matching noise or by beating an already-good average.

For biologists

Failures cluster where cells lack the receptor, in rare cell types, and in donors whose cells were already interferon-activated.

For the benchmark

Weight items by reliability, report the correlation after removing the average, and flag non-cytokines (Megalin, IL-18Ra).
